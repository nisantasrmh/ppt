The Critical Challenge

Why Current Medical AI Falls Short

⚠️

LLM Hallucinations

Pure generative AI models guess drug-drug interactions (DDIs), risking patient safety.

🛡️

Cloud Privacy Leaks

Uploading raw prescription images with patient PII to remote servers violates compliance.

🧪 4-Stage Clinical Verification Pipeline

RxHandBD → RxNorm → TWOSIDES → Gemma 4

Inspect every deterministic transition in the end-to-end pipeline: from real handwriting OCR tolerance testing to concept grounding, deterministic interaction querying, and few-shot plain-language clinical synthesis.

🔬 STAGE 1

OCR Testing

RxHandBD (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18478741)
Format: Images + CSV (5,578 images, 1,769 medical words)

Test OCR's tolerance on real prescription handwriting and gate <80% confidence items

🧭 STAGE 2

Entity Grounding

RxNorm Concept Table
Format: SQLite / CSV

Map noisy OCR strings and brand aliases to standard active ingredient names and RxCUIs

🛡️ STAGE 3

Verification Engine

DDI Corpus / TWOSIDES
Format: SQLite Table (twosides_ddi)

Deterministic query of interacting drug pairs without LLM hallucination

✨ STAGE 4

Gemma 4 Few-Shot Prompts

n2c2 Medication Event Annotations
Format: JSON (n2c2_few_shot.json)

Context examples showing how to summarize dosages and instructions in plain English
