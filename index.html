The Critical Challenge
1.3M+ annual preventable drug injury events globally

Why Current Medical AI Systems Fall Short

Generic cloud LLMs fail safety audits and regulatory compliance in healthcare.

✍️
01 / Legibility Deficit

Ambiguous Handwriting

Cursive doctor prescriptions routinely lead to critical dosage misinterpretations. Standard OCR either hallucinates or silently ignores unclear dosage units.

⚠️ Example: "Warfarin 2.5mg" read as "Warfarin 25mg" (fatal overdose risk).
⚠️
02 / Hallucination Risk

LLM Guesswork in DDIs

Generative models make plausible-sounding pharmacological claims without authoritative grounding. They fabricate contraindications or miss fatal drug-drug combinations.

⚠️ Example: Missing lethal hypotension in Sildenafil + Nitroglycerin interactions.
🛡️
03 / Privacy Violation

Cloud Privacy Leaks

Streaming raw prescription images with patient names, doctor seals, and phone numbers to US-hosted cloud APIs breaches HIPAA, GDPR, and Indian DPDP Act 2023.

⚠️ Result: Immediate rejection by hospital compliance and insurance underwriters.
The Kernel Panics Thesis: Never use generative LLMs for OCR extraction or DDI guesswork.
